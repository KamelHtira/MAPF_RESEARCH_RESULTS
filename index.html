Small tools for maps and the planner's distance method.

Planner comparison (first results)

One instance per map, 300 steps, 1 s limit per step, 1000 robots (100 on random_32_32_20). Each planner runs unchanged in its own kit. This is a first look: the 10-instance run is still to come.

PIBT with cover points (CP)

The PIBT of Team Hipparcos with one change: the distance to a robot's goal comes from cover points instead of a table of the whole map per goal. Start-up about 0.03 s and 46 to 54 MB per run on the large maps.

Route waypoints (SIPP)

The waypoint planner of the supervisor: SIPP over a reduced map (skeleton), with waypoints along the route. A full planner, not PIBT. Slower per step than CP (13 to 33 ms), with 83 to 119 MB per run on the large maps; one step on sortation_large took 1.02 s.

Team Hipparcos, 2024 submission

The main-round submission of the 2024 competition (PIBT with clustered resumable A*), run in its own 2024 harness with its own task scheduler. It uses about 0.97 s of every 1 s step, and on the large maps it needs 6 to 8 GB of memory and 60 to 80 s of start-up.

Mean planning time per step and peak memory of the whole process (the harness's own memory is included). Hipparcos large-map figures come from 40-step runs, because it needs the whole second every step. The task counts of Hipparcos are not directly comparable: it ran in the 2024 harness with its own task scheduler, while CP and SIPP used the v1.1.5 harness with round-robin assignment. Hipparcos' two runs on the large maps needed its preprocessing limit raised from the harness default of 30 s. On random_32_32_20 the Hipparcos run had 2 vertex conflicts; SIPP had one to three "incorrect vector size" errors in its first steps on the large maps.
