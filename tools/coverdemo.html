CP scores for this goal (D)

Second table: the exact window

Within R steps of the goal (red cells) the CPs are not used. A BFS from the goal gives every cell there its exact distance, and these cells read that number directly. Only cells outside the window use CP slots and D.

Detailed view: every cell as a grid of its CPs

Each slot reads steps + D: the fixed steps from the cell to that CP, plus the CP's own distance to the goal (Dk = score of CPk, unknown until the Dijkstra runs). The smallest sum is the cell's score (yellow). Cells in the exact window are dimmed: they ignore their slots.
