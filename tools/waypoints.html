Map views

Load a map and look at its waypoints, in three views. Cover points are the waypoints of the PIBT planner: every free cell is within C steps of one, and each knows the exact cost from all cells within R steps of it; the tool places them with the same greedy pass as the planner (plain steps, no lanes, so the count can differ a little from a planner run that uses lanes). Route waypoints view shows the thin skeleton of corridors that the SIPP planner builds from the map, and the route waypoints it takes along that skeleton from a start cell to a goal cell (every 24th cell by default). Junction clusters is the area method of Team Hipparcos (2024 competition) with a different choice of base points: instead of a uniform grid, the base points sit on the junctions of the skeleton, no two closer than a chosen distance. Every base point then grows a cluster, all at the same time, and a cell joins the base point that reaches it first. The uniform grid of Team Hipparcos can be switched on for comparison.
