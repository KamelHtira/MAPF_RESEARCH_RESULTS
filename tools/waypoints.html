Waypoint tool

Load a map and look at its waypoints, in three views. Cover points are the waypoints of the PIBT planner: every free cell is within C steps of one, and each knows the exact cost from all cells within R steps of it; the tool places them with the same greedy pass as the planner (plain steps, no lanes, so the count can differ a little from a planner run that uses lanes). Route waypoints view shows the thin skeleton of corridors that the SIPP planner builds from the map, and the route waypoints it takes along that skeleton from a start cell to a goal cell (every 24th cell by default). Skeleton as a graph turns that skeleton into a graph: its dead ends, T junctions and crossings become nodes and the corridors between them become edges. Nodes within a chosen number of steps of a centre node can be merged into one, and the numbers show how big the graph and its tables would be.
